# Hormozi ad research snapshot

Collection date: October 12, 2026. Sample: 1,214 ad records. Baseline: 96/1,214 dated active ads were aged 60+ days (7.9%).

These historical labels were generated with the previous OpenAI connector. They are preserved research data, not new Jev results. The current Ad Lab classifier uses native Jev image inputs.

## Talking head has the highest 60+ day share

58 of 306 dated active ads in this group started at least 60 days earlier. Median age: 19 days. 2.4 times the sample-wide 60+ day share.

## How to read this

Age in an active snapshot is not survival probability, continuous delivery, profitability, CTR, CPA or ROAS. Multiple IDs may repeat a creative. The sample is a public-library snapshot, not proof of complete account coverage. Inspect original ads and weaker examples before choosing a test. Source links may become unavailable.
